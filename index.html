About Us

Our Story

Ajaybi Chai Station is the heartfelt creation of two friends from a remote Indian village. We've transformed the simple act of chai into a vibrant celebration of India's rich culture—evoking roadside adda under neem trees, evening storytelling, galli rituals, and the warmth of community in every authentic cup of masala or cutting chai.

Unlike generic chains, our innovative "Your Chai Station" concept empowers franchise owners with a chef-less model for maximum profits in the segment. We deliver streamlined operations, premium ingredients, consistent recipes, spotless spaces, and full support in training, marketing, and scalable supply chains—taking our Gujarat roots to thriving outlets across cities, towns, and every familiar corner.
